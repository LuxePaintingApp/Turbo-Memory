Our Story

Crafting soundscapes that inspire brave creation

Sonic Canvas began as a late-night experiment between two friends in a warehouse studio. Today it's a global hub for artists and makers hungry for authentic conversations and actionable frameworks.

Manifesto

Create with conviction. Share the process. Protect your energy. Build together. Celebrate every stage.

Meet the hosts

Two creatives with radically different backgrounds united by a shared love for storytelling through sound.

Rhea Solis · Producer & Strategist

Rhea blends brand strategy with sonic design. She has guided Grammy winners, indie filmmakers, and mission-led founders to their signature sound.

Atlas Monroe · Composer & Technologist

Atlas builds tools that help artists experiment faster—from custom synth plugins to adaptive performance rigs for immersive shows.

The journey so far

Our timeline charts the experiments, collaborations, and community rituals that shaped Sonic Canvas into a movement.

2019 · Warehouse beginnings

Started recording around a single mic after midnight sessions.

2020 · Community launch

Released our first workshop series and built an intimate Discord hub.

2022 · Global collaborations

Partnered with artists across four continents for live scoring events.

2024 · Sonic Canvas Labs

Opened a residency space for interdisciplinary creators and educators.
